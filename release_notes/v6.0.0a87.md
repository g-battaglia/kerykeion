# v6.0.0a87 — The house you clicked

a86 is on PyPI and this tree has moved past it. That is the first reason for a new
number: the wheel a86 ships is not the wheel this tree draws, and the license file
the NOTICE points at never made it into the a86 wheel at all — PyPI does not allow
a version to be replaced, so the fix can only reach anyone under a new one.

What changed since is one subject with several faces: **which house you are
looking at, and which one answers when you click it.**

## Six house systems draw their houses backwards

Above roughly 68 degrees Campanus, Regiomontanus, Sunshine, Polich/Page and APC
return their cusps in descending order, and the horizon system does it on the
equator. Counted over 32,844 charts — all 23 systems, half a degree of latitude at
a time, four times of day — six reverse outright and two more go degenerate.

Read forwards, which is all the drawing code knew how to do, a six-degree house
measures 354. The twelve then wind round the wheel eleven times instead of once,
so every one of the twelve transparent hit-wedges was painted as a near-complete
ring: stacked, invisible, each declaring `pointer-events: all`, so a click
anywhere on the chart was answered by whichever was drawn last. The house numbers
went with them, each centred half of 354 degrees along — the far side of the wheel
from the house it names.

Direction is now read once from all twelve, because it cannot be decided pair by
pair: a single house may legitimately run past 180 degrees, which Placidus manages
at high latitude, and taking the shorter arc there would cut it in half. Twelve
widths cover the circle exactly once whichever way the houses run, so the total
tells the two apart — 360 one way, 3960 the other.

## Cusps that cross, and the house with no target

Polich/Page inside the polar circle, and Sunshine/alt, return cusps that are not
ordered at all: one house runs back while the next few run on, so the houses
genuinely overlap and nothing can tile. There each wedge is held to at least one
degree, moving only its own end — overlapping its neighbour, which is honest,
because the cusps overlap.

Two cusps inside one whole degree collapse onto one offset when the classic ring
is quantised, and an arc whose endpoints coincide is dropped by the SVG spec: a
path of no area still declaring `pointer-events: all` is a house nobody can ever
click. They are separated in house order now, so the boundaries stay shared —
wedge i ends exactly where wedge i+1 begins — and three coincident cusps come
apart as readily as two.

Swept over 690 charts, every house system, both engines: no wedge of zero area,
none under the minimum.

## House numbers, measured where they are actually drawn

A label's reach in degrees is its reach in pixels over the arc a degree covers, so
the radius goes straight into the answer. It was being taken at the radius where
the cusp *line* ends rather than where the number is written — 120 against 192 on
a natal wheel, 80 against 156 on a dual chart's inner ring — which made every
extent 1.6x too large, and 1.95x on that inner ring. Per-label extents exist to
stop a crowd being walked out of its own houses; measured there they walked it
further than the uniform figure they replaced.

Two more of the same family: the inner ring took its base from the truncated
offsets its lines are drawn from and its span from the exact positions, so where
two cusps share a degree the fractions decided the order and the wheel read **10
before 9, and 4 before 3**; and the outer ring of a dual chart labelled lines that
keep their fraction with numbers built on a truncated base.

## The composite chart gets a cusp ring it can have

About one couple in sixteen got twelve cusps that were not a house division.
Between two points on a circle there are two midpoints, half a turn apart, and
taking the nearer one for each of the twelve independently breaks when the two
charts' angles are nearly opposed: the choice flips partway round the ring and the
twelve arcs come to 1080 degrees instead of 360.

The trade the profession settles on is to hold one angle at its near midpoint and
move the others onto their far midpoint as the order requires — Solar Fire's
"long-arc midpoints instead of short-arc", Kepler's "flipping the houses 180
degrees if necessary", Townley's rule for the stray cusp and its opposite. The new
`house_anchor` argument chooses which angle is held: `auto` (the default, and Solar
Fire's — whichever of the Ascendant and Midheaven has its base cusps closer
together), `ascendant`, or `midheaven`. A ring that already runs in order comes
back untouched, value for value.

Where both charts' houses run the same way the repaired ring covers the circle
exactly once, always. Where one partner's houses run backwards and the other's
forwards there is no direction for the composite to run in, and that is said out
loud rather than shipped quietly.

The composite also stopped keeping a private copy of the library's house reader.
The copy measured every house as the arc running forwards from its cusp, so on a
descending ring it filed ten points out of ten wrongly — while the same model's
house-comparison field, which used the shared function, disagreed with it.

## Notes for anyone building on the SVG

- No `kr:` attribute, node name, `Literal` or schema field was removed or renamed:
  the node vocabulary is 67 values before and 67 after. A consumer reading
  `kr:node="HouseSector"` and `kr:house` needs no change — the wedges it was
  already reading are simply the right shape now.
- `HouseSector` path data changes on charts with reversed or crowded houses, and
  the `large-arc`/`sweep` flags change with it. Both flags move together: SVG puts
  two circles of a given radius through any two points, and it is the pair that
  says which.
- House number positions move on every chart, by a few pixels where nothing was
  crowded and by more where something was.
- `CompositeSubjectModel` gains `house_anchor`, recording which angle was held.
  `None` on a Davison chart, which never needs one.
- `AstrologicalSubjectFactory` writes its log lines to its own module logger
  instead of the root logger, so a host that silences `kerykeion.*` now does.
- `normalize_degree` and `house_spans` moved to `kerykeion.utilities.core`. Both
  are still importable from `kerykeion.charts.utils`.
- The default fixed-star lists spell Deneb Algedi `Deneb_Algedi`, the form used
  everywhere else and the one that has a translation.
