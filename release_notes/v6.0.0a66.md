# v6.0.0a66

Astrological calendar primitives: the building blocks a printed astrological
calendar is made of — a mundane aspectarian, void-of-course windows over a date
range, and season markers on the Sun's cardinal ingresses.

## Mundane aspectarian

`MundaneAspectFactory` (`kerykeion.mundane_aspects`) returns every exact
transiting-to-transiting aspect within a date range — the aspectarian of a
printed calendar. It samples the signed pairwise separation on a uniform 6-hour
grid and refines each crossing by bisection, which is unconditionally
convergent (Newton diverges on slow mutual pairs near stations). It splits the
step at relative-motion reversals and guards the branch cut against antipode
wraps.

The default scan set is Sun..Pluto with the five Ptolemaic aspects. The Moon is
opt-in — its ~75 events/month are noise for most consumers — and the full
minor-aspect vocabulary from the chart defaults is accepted. Aspect instants are
zodiac-independent (there is a sidereal-invariance test); the reported
longitudes and signs follow the requested zodiac. The result is a
`MundaneAspectsCollectionModel` with per-event longitudes, signs and retrograde
flags.

## Void-of-course windows over a range

`VoidOfCourseMoonFactory.from_iso_range` walks the Moon sign by sign and returns
every void-of-course window intersecting the range (unclipped), each framed by
its opening aspect and closing ingress, as a
`VoidOfCourseWindowsCollectionModel`. It reuses the shipped single-moment Newton
machinery unchanged. Whole-sign voids — a sign the Moon crosses making no aspect
— are reported with `last_aspect: null`.

## Season markers on Sun ingresses

`IngressModel.season_marker` (optional) tags the Sun's cardinal-boundary
ingresses with `march_equinox` / `june_solstice` / `september_equinox` /
`december_solstice`. The names are hemisphere-neutral and month-based; the field
is `None` on every other ingress. Additive and backward compatible.
