# v6.0.0a68

Repin of the default backend to **libephemeris `3.0.0rc6`** with the golden
position fixtures regenerated on it, plus the fresh full-codebase review
(round 48) whose fixes had landed on `alpha/v6` but not yet been released.

## Backend repinned rc3 → rc6, golden fixtures regenerated

`6.0.0a65` pinned `libephemeris==3.0.0rc3`; this alpha repins to `==3.0.0rc6`.
`rc6` is a provenance/independence release: no public API or numeric behaviour
change in the `rc5 → rc6` step itself (the frame-transform path —
`planets.py`, `utils.py`, `sidereal_epoch.py`, `time_utils.py` — is diff-clean
of anything but comments and renames), and both upstream test backends stay
green at 16024 each.

The gap that moved numbers is `rc3 → rc6`, across which the reference-frame
transforms were refined by roughly an arcsecond:

- **sidereal fixed-reference modes** (`J1900`, `J2000`, `B1950`),
- **heliocentric / topocentric / true-geocentric perspectives**.

Spot-check against Swiss Ephemeris (the ground truth), John Lennon Moon in
sidereal `J2000`:

| source                 | abs_pos        | off vs Swiss |
|------------------------|----------------|--------------|
| libephemeris rc6       | 304.37271839   | **0.17″**    |
| previous fixture (rc3) | 304.37313858   | 1.69″        |

`rc6` is ~10× closer to Swiss — the previous fixtures carried the *less*
accurate value. All 41 sidereal-mode and 3 non-default-perspective
expected-position fixtures were regenerated on `rc6` (23 files changed). The
8 tolerance failures this caused on first pin (Moon/Mercury/Pluto, all in
frame-transform paths, all sub-arcsecond) are resolved; the suite is green at
9689 passed. The residual ~0.17″ `libephemeris`-vs-Swiss offset is the known
benign ΔT / long-term-precession model difference.

SVG chart baselines were **not** regenerated: a ~1.5″ position shift is far
below rendering precision (charts place bodies by sign/degree and aspect lines
by orb), so every chart baseline is byte-identical and all 463 chart tests stay
green.

## Test fix

- **`test_defaults_to_current_time_when_none` compared against the wrong
  timezone.** The factory resolves "now" in the subject's own timezone
  (`Etc/GMT` = UTC in the test); the assertion used naive `datetime.now()`
  (local). The test therefore failed whenever the local day differed from the
  UTC day near midnight in a non-UTC local timezone — silently green on UTC CI.
  It now compares against `datetime.now(timezone.utc)`, matching the factory.

## Fresh full-codebase review (round 48)

A from-scratch review that deliberately ignored every previous round's "clean"
verdict. 48 finders, three adversarial skeptics per finding, then an execution
proof for each survivor: a reproduction script, or a mutation test showing the
suite stayed green without it. Findings that could not be reproduced were
dropped, including two raised by the reviewer itself. (Full detail in the
`6.0.0a68` CHANGELOG entry.)

## Astrological calendar primitives (bundled from `6.0.0a66`)

`6.0.0a66` and `6.0.0a67` were tagged on `alpha/v6` but their content sat under
the CHANGELOG's Unreleased section; this alpha is the first release that ships
them. `6.0.0a66` added the astrological-calendar building blocks:

- **`MundaneAspectFactory`** — every exact transiting-to-transiting aspect in a
  date range (a printed calendar's aspectarian).
- **`VoidOfCourseMoonFactory.from_iso_range`** — every void-of-course window
  intersecting a range, framed by its opening aspect and closing ingress.
- **`IngressModel.season_marker`** — Sun ingresses at the cardinal boundaries
  carry `march_equinox` / `june_solstice` / `september_equinox` /
  `december_solstice`.

`6.0.0a67`'s intermediate `libephemeris==3.0.0rc5` pin is superseded by `rc6`
here (`rc5` and `rc6` are numerically identical).
