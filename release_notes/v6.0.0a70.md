# v6.0.0a70

This alpha makes the house meaning of every dual-chart SVG point explicit and
machine-readable without breaking existing consumers.

## Dual-chart house metadata

Every `<g kr:node="ChartPoint">` in a dual wheel now carries:

- `kr:house`: the point's house in its owner's chart, unchanged from previous
  releases;
- `kr:projectedhouse`: the point's house in the other subject's house system;
- `kr:horoscope`: the owner ring (`0` or `1`), unchanged;
- `kr:projectedhoroscope`: the target ring used by `kr:projectedhouse`.

The contract applies symmetrically to both rings of Transit, Synastry,
DualReturnChart (solar, lunar and other double-wheel returns), and Progression
charts. It is identical in classic and modern styles and in full-chart and
wheel-only output.

The projected placement is derived directly from the other subject's twelve
cusps. It therefore remains available when `include_house_comparison=False` or
when the visual comparison tables are hidden.

## Compatibility

This is an additive SVG metadata change. `kr:house` keeps its established owner
house semantics, and chart geometry, labels, aspects and serialized chart data
are unchanged. Consumers that need the house system visibly shared by a modern
dual wheel or the natal house of a transit point can use
`kr:projectedhouse`; consumers presenting each subject's own domification can
continue using `kr:house`.
