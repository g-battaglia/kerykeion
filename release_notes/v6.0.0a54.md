# v6.0.0a54

This alpha adds two sky-event finders: planetary retrograde/direct stations and
zodiac sign ingresses, each scanning a date or Julian-Day range.

## `RetrogradeStationFactory`

`RetrogradeStationFactory` (`kerykeion.retrograde_stations`) finds planetary
retrograde and direct stations — the moments a planet's apparent longitudinal
motion reverses — across a date or Julian-Day range (`from_iso_range` /
`from_julian_day`). It is backend-agnostic: it samples the longitudinal speed
and bisects each sign change to the zero crossing, like `LunationFinderFactory`.
The result is a `RetrogradeStationsCollectionModel` whose `StationModel` items
carry the planet, station type (`SR` / `SD`), UTC timestamp, Julian Day and
zodiac position. The Sun and Moon are excluded, as they never station.
`RetrogradeStationFactory`, `StationModel` and
`RetrogradeStationsCollectionModel` are re-exported from the package root.

## `SignIngressFactory`

`SignIngressFactory` (`kerykeion.sign_ingresses`) finds zodiac sign ingresses —
a body crossing a 30° boundary — across a date or Julian-Day range. Its
`IngressModel` items carry the planet, the from/to signs, a retrograde flag for
re-entries, the UTC timestamp and the Julian Day. It detects multiple crossings
within a single sampling interval (a retrograde re-entry near a station) via a
recursive midpoint probe. The Moon is opt-in. `SignIngressFactory`,
`IngressModel` and `SignIngressesCollectionModel` are re-exported from the
package root.

Both finders are validated against known anchors — the 2026 Mercury retrograde
windows, the solar equinoxes and solstices, and the 2023–2024 Pluto
Capricorn↔Aquarius ingress dance — and support BCE ranges via `from_julian_day`.
